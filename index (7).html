Which Difference-in-Differences Estimator Should I Use?

No single DiD estimator is always right. The right one depends on the treatment path and the estimand you target. This guide diagnoses the design first, then names compatible estimators and their assumptions.

dCDH methods (did_multiplegt_dyn, _stat, _had) cover standard staggered adoption and more general nonbinary/nonabsorbing designs. Stata users can run whichDIDshouldIuse to diagnose their design automatically — see below.

Other modern DiD estimators

Candidates for the staggered-adoption row. None dominates uniformly.

Callaway–Sant'Anna

Group-time ATTs; never- or not-yet-treated comparison, user's choice; doubly robust with covariates.

PaperR: didStata: csdid

Sun–Abraham

Interaction-weighted event study; never- or last-treated comparison.

PaperStata: eventstudyinteract

Borusyak–Jaravel–Spiess

Imputation estimator; fits FE on untreated cells only, then imputes.

Paper

did2s & related

Two-stage imputation in the spirit of Gardner (2021).
